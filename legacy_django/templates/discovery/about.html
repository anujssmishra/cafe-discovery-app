What you can do today

02

Information with context

Listings show their recorded sources and checking dates. Missing information stays unknown. Prices are indicative, and a checking date does not guarantee that a menu or location remains current.

A small location count does not prove independent ownership. Ownership claims need their own evidence. We clearly separate objective checked facts (address, offerings, independent status) from opinions (curator editorial reviews) and matching (how closely a café aligns with your preferences). Your private feedback (saved cafés and visit logs) is never shared publicly.

03

Selection methods & Curator context

Cafés are selected based on their focus on specialty coffee, independent ownership, and community value. Curator notes and ratings (for coffee, experience, and trip value) are clearly marked as opinions. All published text in these reviews is originally authored or explicitly permitted for use on this site.

04

Corrections & Feedback

Our data relies on periodic checks and can become stale. If you notice outdated or incorrect facts for a café, you can use the Suggest a correction link at the bottom of any café's detail page to submit a correction report.
