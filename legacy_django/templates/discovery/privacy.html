Privacy Notice & Support

Effective: October 2026

Pilot Eligibility

This early access pilot is strictly available to adults (18+). By participating, you confirm you meet this eligibility requirement.

What We Collect and Why

  • Account Details: Username and email (if provided) for authentication and support.
  • Preferences & Visits: Taste preferences and visited cafés to provide personalized matches and shortlists.
  • Support Logs: Crash reports and operational logs (kept up to 30 days) to keep the service running.

Third-Party Services

We use OpenStreetMap (OSM) for our catalog map and link out to Google Maps for directions. We do not send your location, preferences, or account identity to these map providers. Map interactions only use generic area coordinates or fixed café locations.

Data Rights & Retention

You have the right to export your data or delete your account at any time from your account settings. Your profile, saves, and private visits are kept while your account is active. If your account is deleted, your personal data is removed from our active systems.

Support & Contacts

If you have questions about your data, or need help with your account, please contact the project owner (Anuj) through the provided pilot feedback channel.
